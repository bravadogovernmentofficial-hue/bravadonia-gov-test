About the Station

From the banks of the Dnipro to the shores of the Detroit River

🇺🇦

Origins

UT3DN began transmitting as Spirit of Dnipro in April 2026. The station’s roots lie in Dnipropetrovs’k, Ukraine, carrying the spirit of Ukrainian music and identity across the airwaves.

🇨🇦

New Home

The transmitter is now located in Windsor, Ontario, Canada. From this new base, UT3DN continues to share Ukrainian music with listeners across North America and beyond.

🎵

Programming

Focused on Ukrainian music — traditional, contemporary, and everything in between. Each broadcast opens with the national anthems of Ukraine and Canada, uniting both cultures on the air.
